GeoStrix - free, open-source 3D software for mineral exploration
https://geostrix.com

PRIVACY
GeoStrix has no accounts and no telemetry. It does not collect or send personal information, usage data or crash reports. Your project data stays on your computer.

GeoStrix only goes online for features you use:
 - map tiles and terrain, when you add a basemap or fetch terrain;
 - your own database or web-map (WMS / WMTS / WFS) servers, when you connect to them;
 - GitHub, to check for a newer version at startup and when you choose Help > Check for Updates. The startup check can be turned off in the Help menu.
These requests show your IP address to the service contacted; nothing that identifies you or your project is sent.

Help > Report a Problem makes a zip file on your computer and opens an email for you to send yourself; nothing is sent automatically.

Full privacy policy: https://geostrix.com/privacy
Code signing policy: https://github.com/Matt-Mendes-ai/geostrix/blob/master/CODE_SIGNING.md

UNINSTALLING
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > GeoStrix > Uninstall (or Control Panel > Programs and Features). Your project files are not deleted.
