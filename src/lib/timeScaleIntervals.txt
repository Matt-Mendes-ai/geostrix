International time scale interval colours (bundled with GeoStrix since TASKS.csv #2690)

What: the 170 intervals of Macrostrat's "international intervals" timescale (eons to ages), each with its name, abbreviation,
type, top and base age (Ma) and colour, as used by Surface Geology > Map layers > Time-scale colours. Nothing else was kept.

Source: Macrostrat API, https://macrostrat.org/api/v2/defs/intervals?timescale=international%20intervals
Downloaded: 2026-11-12, once, and trimmed to the fields above (no values changed). The colours are those of the international
chronostratigraphic chart as Macrostrat publishes them.
